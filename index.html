PhD Candidate · Electrochemical Energy Storage Materials

I am pursuing a doctoral degree at Beijing University of Chemical Technology. My research develops functional nanomaterials for safer, longer-lasting rechargeable batteries, with an emphasis on fast ion transport, stable electrode–electrolyte interfaces, and sustainable material design.

RESEARCH INTERESTS

My work sits at the interface of materials chemistry and electrochemical energy storage.

01 / Advanced electrolytes
Solid polymer and hydrogel electrolytes designed for high ionic conductivity, mechanical stability, safety, and sustainable end-of-life.

02 / Metal-anode interfaces
Materials and interfacial strategies for stable lithium and zinc plating/stripping.

03 / Silicon–carbon anodes
Conductive carbon architectures that improve ion transport and accommodate silicon volume change.

Keywords: solid-state batteries · aqueous zinc batteries · nanofibers · hydroxyapatite · ion transport

Bone-inspired sustainable hydrogel electrolytes

Published online 29 May 2025 · Angewandte Chemie International Edition

Jiaying Peng is the first author of a study introducing Zn-HA-Gel, a bone-inspired hydrogel electrolyte composed of zinc-doped hydroxyapatite nanofibers and a gelatin matrix.

The design combines fast Zn2+ transport with water retention, mechanical strength, recyclability, biodegradability, and stable zinc plating/stripping.
